In the United States, today is Thanksgiving. I thought I would take a moment and say how thankful I am for all the people who have been donating to Bugzilla over the last couple years. While we still don't have enough recurring donations to hire a permanent developer, we **do** finally have enough saved up from the last few years to get specific projects done, and the current one is getting the next release out, which has had major progress made that we wouldn't have made without those donations. Look for more announcements from us over the next week or so.
